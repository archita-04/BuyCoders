# BuyCoders – Requirement Document

## 1. Problem Statement

Online shoppers spend significant time comparing products across multiple e-commerce platforms.  
They face challenges such as:

- Misleading discounts
- Overwhelming number of reviews
- Lack of clear and transparent recommendations
- Platform-biased product suggestions

This leads to poor purchase decisions and reduced user confidence.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution

BuyCoders proposes an AI-powered cross-platform shopping assistant that:

- Collects and stores customer reviews in a structured database
- Filters and analyzes reviews using AI
- Compares product prices across platforms
- Detects fake or inflated discounts
- Recommends products based on user needs and budget
- Provides clear explanations for recommendations

---

## 3. Objectives

- Reduce product research time
- Improve decision-making for users
- Increase transparency in online shopping
- Deliver explainable AI-based recommendations

---

## 4. Target Users

- Students
- Working professionals
- Budget-conscious online buyers

---

## 5. Key Features

- Chat-based smart product search
- Cross-platform price comparison
- AI-based review filtering
- Pros & Cons extraction from reviews
- Transparent ranking system
- Fake discount detection

---

## 6. Scope (Prototype – Hackathon Phase)

- Single product category (e.g., smartphones)
- Pre-collected dataset for products and reviews
- Rule-based ranking with sentiment support
- Basic explainable output

---

## 7. Expected Outcome

- Faster product selection
- Reliable and explainable recommendations
- Improved user trust in online shopping platforms
